# Kakakku Meninggal, Tapi Suaminya Malah Kaya Mendadak

Naskah video panjang (narator Laras, suara perempuan). Perkiraan durasi: ~13 menit, 57 adegan.

Keterangan: 🎙 = kalimat dalam tanda kutip dibacakan suara tokoh berbeda · 💥 = jumpscare · ⏱ = perkiraan detik adegan (termasuk jeda)


## 1. Pembuka

**1.** Tujuh hari setelah kakakku dimakamkan, suaminya membeli mobil baru. Bayar tunai. _(⏱ 10 dtk)_

> Gambar: a man proudly smiling beside a brand new shiny white car parked in front of a modest Javanese village house, withered funeral wreaths still leaning at the gate, grey overcast afternoon

**2.** Namaku Laras. Aku dua puluh tahun, masih kuliah. Ini cerita yang nggak pernah berani kuceritakan ke siapa pun. _(⏱ 14 dtk)_

> Gambar: a young woman sitting alone on a wooden bench on a village house veranda at dusk, looking straight at the viewer with tired eyes, warm orange lamp light behind her

**3.** Kakakku, Mbak Rina, meninggal mendadak. Katanya sakit lambung. Padahal dua minggu sebelumnya, dia masih tertawa sambil menggoreng tempe. _(⏱ 16 dtk)_

> Gambar: warm nostalgic memory: a woman laughing happily while frying tempeh in a wok in a simple village kitchen with a clay stove, soft golden light, steam rising

**4.** Rumah kami ada di ujung kampung. Di belakangnya cuma kebun pisang dan sawah, yang gelap sekali kalau malam. _(⏱ 11 dtk)_

> Gambar: an old Javanese village house at the very edge of the village at night, banana trees and dark rice fields behind it, a single yellow lamp on the porch, moon behind thin clouds

**5.** Dan semua keanehannya, dimulai dari hari pemakaman. _(⏱ 7 dtk)_

> Gambar: a funeral procession on a narrow village path toward a graveyard with frangipani trees, mourners in black carrying a wooden bier with a green cloth, a young woman walking behind them with red swollen eyes


## 2. Kejanggalan

**6.** Hari itu mendung. Semua orang menangis. Ibuku pingsan dua kali. Aku sendiri lemas, bahkan untuk berdiri. _(⏱ 15 dtk)_

> Gambar: a village burial at a graveyard under a heavy grey sky, villagers in black weeping around a fresh grave, an elderly woman fainting and held up by neighbors, a young woman crying in the foreground

**7.** Tapi ada satu orang yang berdiri tegak di samping makam. Mas Dimas, suami kakakku. Wajahnya datar. Matanya kering. _(⏱ 15 dtk)_

> Gambar: a man standing stiff and upright beside the fresh grave with a completely blank cold expression and dry eyes while crowds weep blurred behind him

**8.** Aku lihat sendiri, waktu tanah mulai ditimbun, dia malah melirik jam tangannya. Seperti nggak sabar mau pulang. _(⏱ 12 dtk)_

> Gambar: extreme close-up of a man's face glancing down impatiently at his wristwatch while soil is shoveled behind him at the graveside

**9.** Sepulang dari makam, Mas Dimas langsung mengunci kamar Mbak Rina. Kuncinya dia simpan sendiri. _(⏱ 11 dtk)_

> Gambar: a man in a dim hallway of an old Javanese house locking an old wooden door with a brass key and slipping the key into his pocket, an oil-lamp glow

**10.** Katanya, jangan ada yang masuk, biar kenangan Mbak Rina nggak berantakan. Alasannya masuk akal, tapi nadanya terlalu tegas. _(⏱ 14 dtk)_

> Gambar: a man standing in front of a locked door holding up one hand firmly in a stop gesture while a young woman and an old woman stand behind him looking uneasy

**11.** Malam itu aku nggak bisa tidur. Waktu lewat depan kamar itu mau ke dapur, langkahku berhenti sendiri. _(⏱ 11 dtk)_

> Gambar: a young woman in a dark hallway at night frozen in front of a closed old wooden door, bare feet on cold tile floor, weak yellow bulb flickering overhead

**12.** Dari sela bawah pintu, tercium bau melati. Pekat sekali. Padahal Mbak Rina nggak pernah menanam melati. _(⏱ 13 dtk)_

> Gambar: close-up of the gap under an old wooden door with faint white mist curling out and fresh jasmine petals scattered on the tile floor

**13.** Aku mundur pelan-pelan. Dan sejak malam itu, aku mulai memperhatikan Mas Dimas. _(⏱ 10 dtk)_

> Gambar: a young woman slowly backing away down a dark hallway while a faint orange glow leaks from under the locked door behind her


## 3. Kaya mendadak

**14.** Seminggu kemudian, mobil itu datang. Putih, mengkilap, masih bau plastik. Harganya ratusan juta, dibayar tunai. _(⏱ 15 dtk)_

> Gambar: neighbors crowding around a brand new white SUV in the yard of a village house in daylight, a man holding the car key and smiling smugly, kids touching the shiny car

**15.** Lalu rumah itu direnovasi. Cat baru, keramik baru, pagar tinggi. Tukang bekerja dari pagi sampai malam. _(⏱ 14 dtk)_

> Gambar: construction workers renovating the village house with bamboo scaffolding, stacks of new ceramic tiles, fresh white paint, a tall new fence being built

**16.** Utang Mbak Rina yang dulu bikin dia menangis diam-diam, lunas semua. Hanya dalam satu minggu. _(⏱ 11 dtk)_

> Gambar: a man handing a thick envelope of cash to a stern creditor standing in the doorway of the house, the creditor staring greedily at the money

**17.** Tetangga mulai berbisik. Seorang ibu menarik tanganku, hati-hati, Nduk. Rezeki yang datang tanpa keringat, biasanya ada tagihannya. _(⏱ 15 dtk)_

> Gambar: an old village woman with a headscarf gripping a young woman's wrist and whispering urgently into her ear, the young woman looking frightened

**18.** Aku jadi teringat sesuatu. Sebulan sebelum meninggal, Mbak Rina pernah bicara denganku di dapur, malam-malam, suaranya pelan sekali. _(⏱ 14 dtk)_

> Gambar: memory of two sisters talking in a dim kitchen late at night, the older sister leaning close and whispering with a worried face while the younger listens, one hanging bulb

**19.** Katanya, Mas Dimas sering pergi tiap malam Jumat, tanpa pamit. Pulang menjelang subuh, bajunya bau asap dan bunga. _(⏱ 13 dtk)_

> Gambar: a man's silhouette sneaking out through the village yard gate at midnight carrying a small cloth bundle, moonlight, dark banana trees

**20.** Waktu itu aku cuma bilang, ah, mungkin ikut kumpulan bapak-bapak. Sekarang aku menyesal. Mbak Rina sedang minta tolong, dan aku nggak mendengarnya. _(⏱ 16 dtk)_

> Gambar: a young woman sitting alone on a bed in a dark room holding a framed photo of her older sister, head bowed in regret, tears on her cheeks


## 4. Menginap

**21.** Dua minggu setelah pemakaman, aku kembali ke rumah itu. Ibu belum sanggup pulang, jadi aku yang disuruh membereskan barang-barang Mbak Rina. _(⏱ 14 dtk)_

> Gambar: a young woman with a backpack arriving at dusk at the renovated village house with a new white car in the yard, the house looking too new and cold

**22.** Sore itu Mas Dimas bilang dia ada urusan di luar kota. Aku menginap sendirian. Katanya, anggap saja rumah sendiri. _(⏱ 14 dtk)_

> Gambar: a man driving away in a white car with the window down, smiling oddly at the viewer, a young woman standing on the porch watching him go in the evening light

**23.** Malamnya aku masak mi instan, menonton televisi, sambil sesekali melirik lorong menuju kamar yang terkunci itu. _(⏱ 11 dtk)_

> Gambar: a young woman sitting on the floor at a low table eating instant noodles with the television glowing, glancing nervously toward a dark hallway behind her at night

**24.** Jam sebelas lewat, listrik berkedip. Televisi mati sendiri. Dan semua suara jangkrik di luar, tiba-tiba berhenti. _(⏱ 14 dtk)_

> Gambar: the living room plunged into darkness, television just switched off, a ceiling lamp flickering, a young woman frozen with terror, window showing the dark yard

**25.** Lalu aku mendengar suara perempuan, pelan sekali, memanggil namaku. “Laras... Laras...” Dari arah kamar yang terkunci. 🎙 suara hantu Mbak Rina _(⏱ 18 dtk)_

> Gambar: a young woman standing in the hallway turned toward a locked door at the far end, a thin line of pale light under it, cold mist at her feet

**26.** Aku memberanikan diri berjalan ke lorong. Lantainya dingin. Setiap langkahku terdengar terlalu keras. _(⏱ 12 dtk)_

> Gambar: a young woman walking barefoot down a long dark hallway toward a locked wooden door at the end, her shadow stretching on the wall

**27.** Di depan pintu, ada secangkir kopi pahit yang masih mengepul, dan bunga-bunga di atas piring kecil. Padahal tadi siang nggak ada. Dan ini malam Jumat. _(⏱ 16 dtk)_

> Gambar: close-up of an offering on the floor in front of a locked door: a steaming cup of black coffee and jasmine and kantil flowers on a small plate, a thin trail of incense smoke

**28.** Suara itu memanggil lagi, lebih dekat, seperti tepat di balik pintu. “Laras... tolong aku...” 🎙 suara hantu Mbak Rina _(⏱ 13 dtk)_

> Gambar: a young woman with her face very close to a wooden door listening, tears streaming, the door slightly vibrating with a pale glow along its edges

**29.** Di tengah pintu ada lubang kunci tua dari kuningan. Aku berlutut, menahan napas, dan mengintip. _(⏱ 11 dtk)_

> Gambar: a young woman kneeling at an old wooden door bending her face down to an antique brass keyhole, holding her breath

**30.** Gelap. Gelap sekali. Satu detik. Dua detik. Tiga detik... _(⏱ 11 dtk)_

> Gambar: first-person view looking through an old brass keyhole, a circle of almost complete blackness with a faint dim glimmer deep inside

**31.** Tiba-tiba, ada mata yang balas mengintip dari dalam. 💥 JUMPSCARE _(⏱ 7 dtk)_

> Gambar: extreme close-up through the keyhole of one huge pale white eye staring directly at the viewer from just behind the hole, strands of wet black hair across it, terrifying and uncanny

**32.** Aku terjatuh ke belakang tanpa suara. Mata itu nggak berkedip. Lalu sebuah bisikan, dekat sekali. “Jangan percaya dia.” 🎙 suara hantu Mbak Rina _(⏱ 17 dtk)_

> Gambar: a young woman fallen on the floor scooting backward in horror away from a locked door, pale light seeping from its keyhole, mist swirling

**33.** Paginya, aku mulai membereskan lemari Mbak Rina. Di balik tumpukan kain, ada buku catatan kecil bersampul biru. _(⏱ 13 dtk)_

> Gambar: a young woman pulling a small blue notebook from under stacks of folded clothes in an old wooden wardrobe, morning light

**34.** Catatan itu dimulai enam bulan sebelum dia meninggal. Tentang Mas Dimas yang berubah. Tentang bau kemenyan di bajunya. _(⏱ 13 dtk)_

> Gambar: a hand holding an open blue notebook with pages of neat sloping handwriting, a pressed dried jasmine flower between the pages

**35.** Halaman-halaman berikutnya, tulisannya makin berantakan. Dia menulis, “Mas Dimas bukan lagi suamiku. Ada yang lain di matanya.” 🎙 suara Mbak Rina (tulisan di buku) _(⏱ 17 dtk)_

> Gambar: close-up of shaky messy handwriting scribbled across a notebook page, ink smudged, a trembling thumb holding the paper

**36.** Jumat malam, Mas Dimas pulang. Dia pura-pura ramah, menanyakan aku sudah makan atau belum. Aku pura-pura tidur. _(⏱ 14 dtk)_

> Gambar: a man smiling fakely at the doorway carrying a plastic bag at night, a young woman lying on a sofa with her eyes tightly shut pretending to sleep

**37.** Tengah malam, kudengar bunyi kunci diputar. Aku mengendap ke lorong, dan pintu kamar Mbak Rina sedikit terbuka. _(⏱ 13 dtk)_

> Gambar: a young woman tiptoeing down a dark hallway toward a door that is slightly open with warm orange candlelight and smoke leaking out of the gap

**38.** Dari celahnya, aku melihat Mas Dimas duduk bersila memakai sarung hitam. Di depannya ada sesajen, kopi pahit, dan kemenyan berasap tebal. _(⏱ 15 dtk)_

> Gambar: view through a door crack: a man sitting cross-legged in a dark sarong before a low altar covered with offerings, black coffee, seven-kind flowers, burning candles and thick incense smoke, clearly visible

**39.** Bibirnya komat-kamit, bicara pada kegelapan di depannya. Dan dari kegelapan itu, sesuatu menjawab dengan suara berat, bukan suara manusia. _(⏱ 14 dtk)_

> Gambar: a man chanting before an altar facing a dark corner where a towering shadowy demonic figure with faint glowing red eyes looms inside thick incense smoke, clearly visible and menacing

**40.** Aku mendengar Mas Dimas berkata pelan, “Sudah satu. Tinggal yang kedua. Beri aku sedikit waktu lagi.” 🎙 suara Mas Dimas _(⏱ 13 dtk)_

> Gambar: close-up of a man's face lit by candlelight, eyes narrow and greedy, whispering with a thin cruel smile, incense smoke curling around him

**41.** Aku mundur tanpa suara, lalu membuka halaman paling akhir buku catatan itu. Hanya ada satu kalimat, tintanya hampir menembus kertas. _(⏱ 15 dtk)_

> Gambar: a young woman in a dark guest room reading the last page of a notebook by the dim light of her phone, her hands trembling

**42.** “Kalau aku pergi duluan, jaga Laras. Dia yang berikutnya.” 🎙 suara Mbak Rina (tulisan di buku) _(⏱ 9 dtk)_

> Gambar: close-up of the last page of a notebook with one line of heavy pressed handwriting, ink almost tearing the paper, a single tear drop on it

**43.** Paginya, Mas Dimas membangunkanku dengan wajah yang cerah. Terlalu cerah. Dia membawakan nasi pecel dan teh manis. _(⏱ 14 dtk)_

> Gambar: a man smiling too brightly carrying a tray with rice pecel and sweet tea toward a young woman waking up in bed, morning sunlight, unsettling atmosphere

**44.** Dia bilang aku nggak perlu kembali ke kos. Tinggal saja di sini, biaya kuliahku dia yang tanggung. Aku cuma tersenyum, padahal seluruh tubuhku menjerit. _(⏱ 16 dtk)_

> Gambar: a man and a young woman sitting across a dining table, he smiles warmly with folded hands and a generous gesture, she forces a polite smile with frightened eyes

**45.** Malam itu aku diam-diam mengemasi tas. Dompet, ponsel, dan buku catatan biru itu. Malam ini juga, aku pergi. _(⏱ 15 dtk)_

> Gambar: a young woman quickly packing a backpack in a dim room by lamplight, a blue notebook on the bed, glancing at the door

**46.** Aku membuka pintu kamar pelan-pelan. Lorong itu gelap. Lalu aku melihatnya. Di ujung lorong, berdiri seorang perempuan. _(⏱ 15 dtk)_

> Gambar: a long dark hallway, at its far end a ghostly woman in a long white dress standing perfectly still, a young woman in the foreground seen from behind frozen at her bedroom door

**47.** Rambutnya panjang, basah, menutupi wajahnya. Dasternya putih, meneteskan air ke lantai. Tapi aku tahu itu Mbak Rina. _(⏱ 15 dtk)_

> Gambar: medium shot of the ghost of a woman in a soaked white dress with long wet black hair covering most of her face, water dripping onto the tile floor, cold mist around her feet

**48.** Dia nggak mendekat. Dia hanya menggeleng pelan. Sekali. Dua kali. Seolah bilang, jangan lewat pintu depan. _(⏱ 15 dtk)_

> Gambar: the ghostly woman with hair over her face slowly shaking her head side to side in a dark hallway, one pale hand lifted gently pointing toward the back of the house

**49.** Dari ruang tamu terdengar langkah Mas Dimas mendekat. Panik, aku berlari ke kamar mandi dan mengunci pintunya. _(⏱ 12 dtk)_

> Gambar: a young woman rushing into an old village bathroom with a water basin, concrete tiles and a dim bulb, locking the door behind her with panic

**50.** Aku menatap cermin di atas bak. Wajahku pucat sekali. Lampu mulai berkedip, dan tengkukku terasa dingin. _(⏱ 13 dtk)_

> Gambar: a young woman looking into a small bathroom mirror above a concrete water basin, her pale reflection, the bulb flickering, a faint dark shape behind her shoulder

**51.** Dan di cermin itu, tepat di belakang bahuku, ada wajah pucat Mbak Rina. 💥 JUMPSCARE _(⏱ 8 dtk)_

> Gambar: bathroom mirror reflection: a young woman's frightened face and right behind her shoulder the deathly pale face of a ghost woman with wet black hair parted just enough to show hollow dark eyes, very close to her ear, terrifying

**52.** Dia nggak menakutiku. Matanya sendu. Bibirnya bergerak, pelan sekali. “Lewat dapur. Sekarang.” 🎙 suara hantu Mbak Rina _(⏱ 17 dtk)_

> Gambar: calm sorrowful close-up of the ghost woman's pale face beside a young woman's shoulder in the mirror, hair slightly parted showing sad eyes, soft cold light


## 7. Penutup

**53.** Aku berlari lewat dapur, kebun pisang, dan sawah yang gelap, tanpa menoleh. Pagi itu, kuceritakan semuanya pada ibu. Ibu menangis lama. _(⏱ 17 dtk)_

> Gambar: a young woman running across a dark rice field at night past banana trees, moonlight, backpack bouncing, the house far behind her with a faint pale figure at a window

**54.** Seminggu kemudian, Mas Dimas mengirim uang dalam jumlah besar ke rumah bibi. Katanya, tanda sayang. Ibu menerimanya, karena nggak tahu apa-apa. _(⏱ 17 dtk)_

> Gambar: an elderly woman at the door of a small village house holding a thick envelope of cash handed by a delivery rider, her face confused and grateful, dusk

**55.** Sejak malam itu, ibu mulai sakit. Demamnya naik turun, matanya cekung, dan tiap malam Jumat dia memanggil nama Mbak Rina. _(⏱ 14 dtk)_

> Gambar: an elderly woman lying weak in bed with sunken eyes and a feverish sweat, a candle on the bedside, scattered jasmine petals on the pillow, quiet sad mood

**56.** Kemarin pagi, di depan pintu rumah bibi, tempat kami sekarang bersembunyi, aku menemukan secangkir kopi pahit. Dan setangkai melati. _(⏱ 14 dtk)_

> Gambar: a small plate with a cup of black coffee and a single jasmine flower on a doorstep at dawn, mist, still and quiet

**57.** Aku nggak tahu siapa yang menaruhnya. Dan aku nggak tahu... giliran siapa yang sedang dipanggil. _(⏱ 10 dtk)_

> Gambar: a young woman sitting alone by a window at dawn, hands around her knees, looking out at the misty yard where a faint pale figure in white stands far away among the trees
